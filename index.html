A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is EMPWR HVAC part of EMPWR Solar?

EMPWR HVAC is EMPWR’s heating and cooling vertical. EMPWR Solar continues to serve solar customers, while EMPWR HVAC focuses on home heating and cooling projects.

Do you serve my area?

Availability depends on your location and our installation partner coverage. Contact us with your city and ZIP code so we can confirm whether we can support your project.

What information should I send?

Your city and ZIP code, the type of system you have, its approximate age, and the issues you’re experiencing are a helpful starting point. A photo of the equipment label can also help identify your current system.

Can you help me choose a replacement?

Yes. The conversation starts with your current setup and comfort needs. The proposed system and installation scope should be reviewed for your home before you commit.
